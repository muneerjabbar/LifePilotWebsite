Dashboard

Manage & calls

Tasks, reminders, follow-ups, schedule phone calls, call trees, favourite contacts & groups — with local alerts.

Important dates, goals, notes, journal (with photos), menstrual calendar, document vault, home, and vehicles.

Compose a message to multiple contacts or groups and open your Messages app to send — listed for everyone, Pro to use.

No LifePilot login. Optional PIN lock. Your data stays on your phone. Pro backup goes to your Google Drive.
