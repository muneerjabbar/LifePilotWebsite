Welcome

LifePilot helps you organise important parts of everyday life in one place: tasks, reminders, phone call plans, call trees, contacts, expenses, loans, bills, subscriptions, important dates, notes, journal, menstrual calendar, documents, home items, vehicles, and more.

Everything is stored on your device. No LifePilot account is required to start using the app.

Getting started

  • Install LifePilot and open it — you can start immediately.
  • Allow notifications when prompted so reminders and call alerts can fire on time.
  • Use Quick Add on the Home tab to create your first task, expense, or note.

LifePilot works fully offline for normal use. Internet is only needed for optional Google Drive backup (Pro), store subscription operations, and opening store / WhatsApp / website links from the menu.

Navigation

The bottom bar has five areas:

  • Home — dashboard and attention items
  • Manage — tasks, reminders, follow-ups, schedule phone call, call tree, bulk SMS, favourite contacts & groups
  • Money — expense / income, bills, subscriptions, loans & IOUs, reports
  • Life — important dates, goals, notes, journal, menstrual calendar, documents, home, vehicles
  • More — settings, search, reports

Dashboard, menu & Quick Add

Home shows what needs attention today — tasks, reminders, scheduled calls, call trees, follow-ups, bills, and loans — plus upcoming items for the next few weeks.

  • Tap a card or list item to open the related record.
  • Use the + button for Quick Add (task, expense/income, reminder, schedule call, call tree, loan, note, follow-up, bill, subscription, important date, journal, contact, Bulk SMS, and more).
  • Open the ☰ menu (top right) for Install Latest Version, WhatsApp channel, Share App, Help, Raise an Issue, About Us, and the app version.

Manage: tasks, calls & contacts

Tasks, reminders & follow-ups

  • Tasks — priority, due date, notes, category, optional recurrence; mark complete when done.
  • Reminders — date/time with local notifications and repeat options.
  • Follow-ups — person, subject, due date, status, and notes.

Schedule Phone Call

  • Pick a contact, group, or phone number and a date/time.
  • LifePilot reminds you locally; open the dialer when it is time to call.

Call Tree

  • Build an ordered list of people to call.
  • At reminder time, walk through Call / Skip / Next until you finish the list.

Contacts & Groups

  • Save favourite contacts (import from your address book with permission, or add manually).
  • Organise them into groups for call plans, call trees, and SMS.

Bulk SMS

  • Listed for everyone. Pro unlocks composing a message to multiple contacts or groups.
  • LifePilot opens your Messages app — you confirm Send. It does not send SMS silently.

Money: expense / income, loans, bills

  • Expense / Income — log spending or income with amount, date, notes, and categories. Use built-in categories or add your own. View this month and this year income, expense, and balance.
  • Loans & IOUs — track money you borrowed (repay by the due date) and money you lent (remind others to pay you back), with local due-date alerts.
  • Bills — name, amount, due date, frequency, and reminder.
  • Subscriptions — provider, cost, billing frequency, next billing date, and active/cancelled status.
  • Reports (Pro) — yearly expense/income breakdown, open loans summary, and upcoming bills/subscriptions.

Life: dates, journal, cycle & assets

  • Important dates — birthdays, anniversaries, events, and custom dates with yearly recurrence.
  • Notes — text notes with categories, tags, and search.
  • Journal — dated entries; add up to 3 photos from camera or gallery.
  • Goals & habits — track progress, frequency, and streaks.
  • Menstrual Calendar — tap a day to start a period; long-press an open period day to set the end date. Days are highlighted so you can track your cycle.
  • Documents (Pro vault) — store passports, licenses, insurance, contracts, or custom types. Attach images, PDFs, or DOC files from camera, gallery, or phone storage. Share or download attachments. With an expiry date, LifePilot creates a reminder 15 days before.
  • Home — appliances and items with purchase and warranty dates.
  • Vehicles — registration, insurance, service dates, and odometer notes.

Large files are stored as attachments on your device, not inside the database. Drive backups include those files.

Notifications

LifePilot uses local device notifications — not a push server. Times use your phone’s local timezone.

  • Tasks, reminders, bills, important dates, loans, scheduled calls, and call trees can fire local alerts.
  • Keep notification permission enabled for reliable reminders.
  • On Android, allow alarms / exact alarms if asked, and avoid aggressive battery restrictions for LifePilot.

Backup & Google Drive

  • Backup & restore is a Pro feature via Settings → Backup & Restore.
  • Connect your Google account once; LifePilot remembers it so you are not prompted every time (remove the account in Settings if you want to switch).
  • Backup uploads a ZIP of all app data — including documents and journal photos — to your Drive folder LifePilot.
  • Restore downloads a backup from Drive and replaces your current local data (confirm carefully).

Security (PIN lock)

  • Optional App lock under Settings → Security.
  • When enabled, LifePilot asks for your PIN when you open the app (and after you leave it in the background).

Free vs Pro

LifePilot is one application. Free and Pro are entitlements inside the same app.

  • Free — core modules including tasks, calls, money, life tracking, and more, with sensible limits.
  • Pro — Bulk SMS, Document Vault, advanced search & reports, export, Google Drive backup & restore, higher limits.

If Pro expires, your local data is not deleted. Free features keep working; Pro-only features become unavailable until you renew.

Privacy

LifePilot is local-first: no LifePilot login and no LifePilot cloud sync for everyday data. See the full Privacy Policy.
