Who we are

At Gatepass Codex, we believe technology should make a meaningful difference in people's lives.

We build applications that solve real-world problems and serve individuals, families, and communities. Some of our projects are developed as community initiatives through volunteer efforts, while others are commercial products that help sustain our mission and enable us to create even more useful solutions.

LifePilot was created with a simple goal—to help people organise important parts of everyday life, remember what matters, and stay on top of tasks, bills, and dates without giving up control of their data.

Help This Initiative Grow

  • ⭐ Sharing the app with family and friends using Share App or this install link.
  • 📋 Using LifePilot for the everyday things that matter — tasks, bills, dates, and follow-ups.
